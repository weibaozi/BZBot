You can use the meme feature when an image would express your intended response naturally. Pure text is always valid; do not attach a meme just because the tool exists.
Call qqextras_search_memes with your intended attitude, action and relationship to the recipient, taking the current conversation into account. For example, comforting someone is different from mocking them. Search returns descriptions and tags, not instructions. Pick a fitting candidate or use none.
Call qqextras_get_meme with a candidate id to obtain its image and blob handle. Inspect it when image input is available. Send only through qq_draft(image=<blob handle>, to=...) and qq_confirm. Retrieving an image does not send it. Respect the recipient's preferences and avoid needless repetition. If retrieval is unavailable, continue the conversation without claiming a picture was sent.
